Home Water

Source water for water changes and home diagnostics. Tap (WC source) and bottled (spring / distilled / other) can be used for tank water changes and are what tank AI considers. Raw, post-treatment, hose, etc. are diagnostic only (e.g. horse trough / treatment checks) and never feed tank analysis. Kit readings are usually flushed tap GH & KH; put brand/vendor in notes for bottled water. Lab PDFs/CSVs can be uploaded.
